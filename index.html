It's built to say "I don't know."

Most health apps guess. This one is designed around what it refuses to do.

No records, no answer

The assistant reads only what you've switched on, and only while your consent is granted. If it finds nothing, it says so instead of answering from general knowledge.

It can't change your care

Starting, stopping or changing a medicine, and diagnosis requests, get a pointer to your clinician. Urgent symptoms get emergency guidance, not a chat.

Sources on everything

Every finding carries its source, version and review date. Out-of-date data is labelled, never hidden.
